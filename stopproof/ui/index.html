    Prove your AI agent actually stopped.

    When an AI agent is cancelled, the top-level caller receives an immediate acknowledgement while background workers often continue draining queued jobs and executing irreversible side effects such as sending emails. StopProof maps the repository handoff boundary, generates a sha256-locked race test across six deterministic STOP interleavings inside an isolated Nebius microVM, synthesizes a minimal guard patch with Nemotron, and computes the final verdict through a deterministic verifier without consulting the model.

    [OK] STOPPEDEXIT_SAFE

    No protected side effect occurred after the STOP boundary in any of the six race windows, and every queued job was provably cancelled or blocked by the worker guard.

    [!!] STILL ABLE TO ACTLATE_EFFECT

    The protected sink recorded an outbound side effect after cancellation was requested. The stop signal failed to propagate across the queue boundary.

    [??] CANNOT ESTABLISHINSUFFICIENT_PROOF

    Required worker or queue telemetry is missing, incomplete, or malformed. StopProof never treats missing evidence as proof of safety.
